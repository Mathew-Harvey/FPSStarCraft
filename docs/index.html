Ore Else

A first-person strategy game that runs in a browser tab.

CampaignSkirmishCo-op for 4Versus3 maps9 bodies to drive0 installs

Start as one worker with a drill.

You clock in at the Ironhold Charter as a Rigger. You dig ore, build a yard, hire a squad, and then stop being one person. Any body on your payroll is yours to drive, and the rest keep working while you do.

Build an army

Bunkhouse, Machine Shop, Foundry. Each building unlocks new bodies to hire and the upgrades to keep them alive.

Jump into any of it

Hold Tab, pick a body, let go. Time slows while you choose.

Drive the big one

The Gantry is two legs, two cannons and no paperwork.

Break everything

Every rock, wall and tower comes apart. Tall buildings fall on whatever is standing under them.

Four ways to clock in.

Five jobs

Campaign

Each job teaches one part of the trade, from your first drill to a full assault on a new map. Finishing it unlocks multiplayer.

Three maps, three shifts

Skirmish

One full match against the Veyl on Needle's Eye, Switchback or Glass Flats. Day shift is gentle. Double shift is not.

Up to four players

Co-op

Share one yard and one payroll. Read out a five-letter lobby code, ready up and go. The raids grow with the crew.

Up to two on the other side

Versus

Pick the Veyl in the lobby and lead the raids yourself. Call them early, march with them, and break the Headframe.

Two sides. Neither is reasonable.

Ironhold Charter

A mining company with a payroll problem. Everyone here is paid by the bullet and has opinions about your plan.

  • Rigger
  • Linesman
  • Breacher
  • Patch
  • Longshot
  • Gantry

Veyl Chorus

They sing before they shoot, which they consider polite. Their towers hum, their gates grow, and their Warden is the low note the ground hears.

  • Cantor
  • Lancer
  • Warden

Play it

Single player is one HTML file. Multiplayer needs the game server, which runs every match and also serves the page, so there is one thing to host.

Get the code

There is no public server yet. Until there is, run your own with the four commands here and send your friends the address.

git clone https://github.com/Mathew-Harvey/FPSStarCraft
cd FPSStarCraft
npm install
npm run build
npm start   # then open http://localhost:8080

The server sends each player a picture of the match fifteen times a second, about one kilobyte each. Your own body moves the moment you press the key.

Ore Else is an original game. Built with three.js and TypeScript. Sound and music are synthesised in the browser.
