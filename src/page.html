Ore Else

Start as one worker with a drill. Build an army that argues with you. Jump into any body you own.

Split screen puts two players on this machine, on the map and shift picked above. Co-op shares whichever side is picked above. In versus, player one is Ironhold and player two leads the side Ironhold is up against, and both sides dig, build and train. Player one keeps the keyboard and mouse. Player two uses a controller, or the right-hand side of the keyboard if there is none.

Joint operations

Two to four players share one Ironhold yard against the Veyl. One of you creates a lobby and reads out the five-letter code.

Lobby

The three sides

IronholdFew, expensive, long-armed. Wins by digging faster and mixing a squad: shield in front, rifles in the middle, a Longshot behind. Weak when caught alone or out of ore.
VeylMany, free, close-in. Wins by never letting the crew settle: Blades tie up Breachers, Lances wear down a Gantry, the Deep Voice walks through a shield wall. Weak in the open against a mixed squad, and every lost Stone thins the raids.
GloamCheap, fast, and always more of them. Every unit ripens from a Bud on a Mother Cap, every building costs a Grazer, and buildings only grow on the Bloom carpet, where the swarm runs faster and heals. Wins by trading badly and winning anyway. Weak to splash, and to anyone who burns the Caps.
