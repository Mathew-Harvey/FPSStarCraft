What makes the look

  • Ink outlines on everything, drawn from the scene's depth and surface angles, thicker up close, with a slight hand wobble.
  • Flat comic colour in three steps: highlight, base, and a cool purple shadow with a ragged edge.
  • Cross-hatching in the shadows, the way a comic artist would shade with a pen.
  • Hand-painted surfaces: panel lines, chipped paint, scratches, rivets and stencils inked onto every material.
  • Chunky cartoon proportions: big helmets, big boots, big shoulders, so a unit reads from across the map.
  • A rust desert under a teal sky, so yellow Ironhold steel and glowing Veyl teal both jump off the background.
  • A comic-book HUD: slanted ink panels, hard drop shadows, speech-bubble callouts, title cards, and a classic RTS overhead view when you hold Tab.

The characters and buildings here are stand-ins assembled from simple shapes in code, so judge the shading, colour, proportions and HUD rather than the modelling detail. Final models would be sculpted properly and dropped into this same shader.

Ore Else is a working title for an original game. Its factions, the Ironhold Charter and the Veyl Chorus, and all units and art are original. Full rules are in the design document in the repository under docs/design.
